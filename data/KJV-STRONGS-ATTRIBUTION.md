# KJV Strong's and morphology data

`kjv-strong-morphology.db` was generated from the CrossWire KJV OSIS source. It preserves the tagged English word or phrase, its Strong's identifier(s), and any supplied morphology code.

Source: [CrossWire KJV2011 OSIS](https://www.crosswire.org/~dmsmith/kjv2011/). The KJV SWORD module is published by CrossWire with embedded Strong's numbers and morphology; retain its attribution when redistributing derived data.

Rebuild with `python scripts/import_kjv_strongs.py`.
