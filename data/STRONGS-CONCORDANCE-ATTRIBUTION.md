# Strong's Concordance lexicon

`strongs-concordance.db` contains every entry in the Open Scriptures digital edition of James Strong's Greek and Hebrew/Aramaic dictionaries. Each entry includes its Strong's code, original-language lemma, transliteration, pronunciation, derivation, concise definition, and KJV renderings.

Source: [Open Scriptures Strong's Dictionaries](https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs). The Open Scriptures JSON digitisation is **CC BY-SA**; the underlying Strong's dictionaries are public domain. Retain the attribution and CC BY-SA obligations when redistributing the generated database or derivatives.

This is an independently licensed Strong's reference work, not a copy of Bible Hub's Lexical Summary pages.

Rebuild with `python scripts/import_strongs_lexicon.py`.
